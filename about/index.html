ABOUT RED BULL

Our Story

Red Bull was born from a unique idea when Dietrich Mateschitz discovered a functional drink on a business trip to Thailand. This discovery led to the creation of a completely new product category - energy drinks.

Since its launch in 1987, Red Bull has become the world's leading energy drink, available in over 171 countries. But we're more than just a drink - we're a lifestyle brand that supports athletes, artists, and innovators who push the boundaries of human potential.

Our mission is to give wings to people and ideas. Through our ownership of football clubs, Formula 1 teams, and extreme sports events, we continue to embody the spirit of energy, excitement, and excellence.
